Carolyn DiLoreto is a multi-media artist and Product Designer at Apple, raised in San Jose, CA in a Filipino-Italian-American family. She is a proud graduate of USC's School of Cinematic Arts, where she studied Media Arts + Practice with minors in Dance and Computer Programming. At Apple, she works within Commerce & Subscriptions, crafting experiences that are accessible, intuitive, and consistent across the ecosystem.

Outside of her day job, Carolyn takes on freelance photography projects across digital and film formats, with clients including the Academy of Motion Picture Arts & Sciences. She also continues to explore movement & dance, a lifelong practice that first shaped her path into visual storytelling. This background in performing arts, media, and technology has cultivated an earnest curiosity for exploring what emerges when these diverse disciplines come together.

Carolyn lives in Los Angeles with her Shiba Inu, finding time for photography, camping, and creating DJ mixes with friends. In all the work she does, she stays mindful of its impact, carrying a steady awareness of the communities her work touches. She's always open to new projects and collaborations, whether that means picking up new skills or putting her own to use. Her photography can be found on Instagram at [@carodilophoto](https://www.instagram.com/carodilophoto).
