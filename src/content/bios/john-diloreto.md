John DiLoreto has served for over 30 years as an engineer, marketer, executive, entrepreneur in the fields of telecom, displays and digital media. As an industry analyst, he has made regular contributions to technology and forecast reports, and has presented at and moderated panels at industry events. With advanccd analytical and business management skills and with senior executive experience in several high-tech start-ups, his expertise in offshore business development, strategic partnering, manufacturing, finance and administration have made him a well-rounded and valuable business consultant and advisor. 

As an industry executive, he has made key contributions in medical imaging, including the introduction of the first artificial intelligence-based medical image processing system. An innovator in large-scale displays, he co-founded Jenmar Visual Systems and holds key patents in refractive screen technology and led this technology into commercialization in a number of markets. Jenmar screens cover the exterior of the landmark Nasdaq building at Times Square and have been installed in the cockpits of F-18 aircraft and on rides at Disneyland. John also has served on the advisory boards of a number of high-tech companies across the country. 

Mr. DiLoreto holds technical and business degrees from M.I.T., U.C. Berkeley and Stanford. He has proven leadership in commercializing new technologies and has a consistent record of rapidly and solidly positioning companies with strategic customers, suppliers, manufacturing partners and the media to entrench new products in established and developing markets. 

On the artistic side, John has been a performing musician on voice and keyboards. Classically trained, he competed on piano in high school and excelled at trumpet in symphony band and orchestra. In his junior year of college he studied at L'Ecole National de Musique in Nantes, France and wrote and performed a score to a theatrical production of Saint-Exupery's "Le Petit Prince." In Palo Alto, John opened Silver Label Recording studio and produced multi-track remote recordings of TheatreWorks and other local theater productions as well as demos of local artists. He also produced and distributed records of local musicians, including Michael Silversher and a joint project with Windham Hill Records and guitarist Robbie Basho. For over 15 years John also performed keyboards and vocals in a number of bands, featuring classic rock and Motown. He has also played keyboard in local community theatre pit orchestras. He has also designed sound for over 100 community theatre productions, in addition to serving as the resident head sound engineer for Tabard Theatre. 

John has worked as a board member of arts-oriented non-profit corporations including the Northern California Songwriters Association, Tapestry Arts, and The Tabard Theatre Company. John has also been CFO for a number of arts and technology companies including Miso Robotics.
